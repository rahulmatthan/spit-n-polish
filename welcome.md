# Welcome to Spit n Polish

You've written the piece. This is where you give it the spit and polish — without anyone rewriting it for you. Everything you type is saved as you go, as a plain markdown file in your Spit n Polish folder, and nothing an AI suggests ever enters your text without a click from you.

Try the right-click menu on any word, sentence or paragraph. Choose **Versions…** and write another way of saying it — then hover over it and press the up and down arrows to flip between the versions in place, reading each one in context before you commit.

Not sure a sentence belongs? Select it and press ⌘' to ghost it. %%This sentence is ghosted: it stays in the draft, faded, out of the word count, until you revive it or delete it for good.%% Ghosted text is kept as an Obsidian-style comment, so the file still opens cleanly in other editors.

Offcuts you can't bear to lose go in the Overflow panel (⌘.), and ⌘⇧. sends the selection straight there. The Structure view (⌥⌘O) shows the piece as paragraph cards you can drag into a new order.

The Lab, at the bottom of the screen, runs editing passes over the whole draft or just the selection: typos, the weakest sentences, convoluted ones, hedges and filler, and trims of ten to fifty per cent that propose cuts for you to accept one by one. They run on your own Claude account — see Settings (⚙, top right).

Press ⌘O to start a new document, open another, or import a markdown file. Press ⌘/ for the full guide. When you're ready, delete this one.
