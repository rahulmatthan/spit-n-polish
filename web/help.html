Spit n Polish — everything it does

For the spit and polish on a piece you've written — without anyone rewriting it. Your documents are plain markdown files in a folder on your computer. Nothing an AI writes enters the draft without a click from you.

Nothing matches.

Getting in

Opening it

Spit n Polish runs quietly in the background on your own computer (if you installed it as a service) and lives at http://127.0.0.1:4848/ — bookmark it, or give it a keyboard shortcut. Nothing is online; there is no account.

Reopens where you were

It opens the document you last had open.

Documents

⌘O or click the name at the top. Lists your documents, newest first; type to filter, ↑↓ and ⏎ to pick. Type a name that doesn't exist and press ⏎ to create it, or click + New.

Import

Import… in the document list, or drop .md files anywhere on the page. Pick X.alts.json or X.overflow.md alongside X.md and they come with it.

Rename · Download · Delete

At the foot of the document list, for the open document. Download gives the .md with ghosted text kept as %%…%%; Download clean leaves it out.

Settings

⚙ at the top right: how the AI is reached, the model for the deeper passes, notes on your voice, and where your documents are.

This guide

⌘/ or the ? at the bottom right.

The screen

Word & character count

Top left. Characters include spaces and punctuation, as Word counts them; line breaks don't count. Ghosted text is left out of both.

Polishing marks on / off

Click the count. Off hides every underline, ghost mark, Lab mark and panel button — a plain editor. Remembered next time.

Status

Top right: editing, saving…, saved, conflict, or why a command couldn't act (e.g. "Can't: already the first sentence").

Bottom bar

Versions (left) · Structure and Lab (middle) · Overflow and ? (right).

Versions (alternatives)

Make versions

Select a word, sentence or paragraph and press ⌘; (or right-click → Versions…). With nothing selected it takes the word at the cursor. The Versions panel opens with a box to write another version.

The add box

Starts with the current wording, cursor at the end, so you can amend rather than retype. ⏎ adds it and makes it the one in the draft; ⇧⏎ new line; Esc back to the draft. A half-typed version survives if you click away.

Cycle in place

Hover over it and press ↑ ↓, or with the cursor in it ⌥↑ ⌥↓, or click a version in the panel.

How they look

Word: wavy underline. Sentence: dotted underline. Paragraph: a rule in the left margin. The underline turns your accent colour when the version showing isn't your original. Hovered or current: lightly highlighted.

Level

Guessed from the selection; change it with Word / Sentence / Paragraph at the top of the panel.

a / an

Swapping a word fixes the article before it ("a paperclip" → "an eraser").

Edit a version

Just type in the draft — you're editing whichever version is showing.

Remove

× beside a version in the panel. Removing the last one removes the set.

◆ AI versions

In the panel, or right-click → AI versions. Added beside yours, marked ◆, never replacing your text. Words: 6 options from Sonnet (a few seconds). Sentences: 3, paragraphs: 2, from the model chosen in Settings, following your voice notes. Never repeats one you already have. "clear AI versions" removes the ones not in use.

Panel

⌥⌘V or Versions at the bottom. With the cursor outside any versions it lists the keys.

Found by their text

Versions are anchored by their text and its surroundings, not by position, so editing the file in another editor (Obsidian, say) doesn't break them. If a versioned passage is rewritten beyond recognition, its set is kept untouched.

Earlier wordings (sentence history)

Automatic

When you reword a sentence by typing and move off it, the old wording is kept — if at least two words changed. Typo fixes, cycling versions, Lab edits, moves and undo don't count.

Where to find them

Cursor in the sentence, Versions panel open: "Earlier wordings", newest first. Hover for the date. Nothing shows in the draft.

use · ×

use brings a wording back as a version (your current one stays as a version too, so you can cycle between them). × forgets it.

Kept

The last 20 per sentence, saved with the document, so they last between sessions.

Ghosting

Ghost / revive

⌘' ghosts the selection — it stays visible, faded, but is no longer part of the piece. ⌘' in a ghost revives it. Also on the right-click menu.

In the file

Ghosted text is wrapped in %%…%% — an Obsidian comment, hidden in Obsidian's reading view if you take the file there.

Left out of

Word and character counts, every AI call and Lab pass, and Download clean.

Overflow

Panel

⌘. or Overflow at the bottom. A free notepad for offcuts, lines you like, notes. Saved with the document.

Stash

⌘⇧. (or right-click → Stash in overflow) cuts the selection from the draft and adds it to the end of the overflow.

Moving & splitting

Move a sentence

⌥⇧↑ ⌥⇧↓ swaps the sentence at the cursor with its neighbour, within its paragraph; the cursor rides along. Press repeatedly to take the last sentence to the top.

Move a paragraph

⌃⌥↑ ⌃⌥↓ swaps the paragraph with the one above or below. Headings and lists count as paragraphs; front matter never moves; a ghost spanning paragraphs moves as one.

Split a sentence

⌥⏎ ends the sentence at the cursor: drops a comma, semicolon or dash there, adds a full stop and capitalises the next word. Mid-word, it splits before the word.

What travels

Versions, earlier wordings and Lab marks inside a moved sentence or paragraph go with it. A move that would cut a version in two is refused.

Sentence ends

"Dr.", "e.g.", "a.m.", initials and similar don't end a sentence; a ghosted sentence counts as one.

Structure view

Open

⌥⌘O or Structure at the bottom. Esc or ⌥⌘O to go back.

Cards

One per paragraph: the first sentence in full, the rest faded, with its number and word count. Headings in bold; ghosted paragraphs struck through.

Reorder

Drag a card. The draft changes and saves; versions and history go with the paragraph.

Jump

Click a card to go to that paragraph in the draft.

◆ Reverse outline

One line per paragraph on what it does in the argument, with its role — opening, claim, context, evidence, example, counterpoint, turn, implication, close. ~20 s. Lines stay with their cards when you reorder; the view tells you how many paragraphs changed since, and "Redo the outline" refreshes it. Shown only here — never in the draft.

The Lab

Open

Lab at the bottom. Works on the selection if you've selected 3+ words, otherwise the whole draft (the menu says which).

Fix punctuation and typos

Each fix shown struck through with the correction beside it. Accept · Skip · Accept all. Sonnet.

Mark the weakest sentences

Up to 6, worst first, each with a one-line note (hover).

Mark sentences that run long

Over 35 words. Instant, no AI, no key needed.

Mark convoluted sentences

Hard to follow on first reading. Up to 6.

Mark words that don't fit the tone

Jargon, needless formality, slang, anything off your voice notes. Up to 10.

Mark hedges and filler

Up to 12.

Voice check

Marks sentences that break the notes on your voice you wrote in Settings, naming the principle each one breaks. Up to 8.

Stepping through marks

‹ › in the Lab bar; click a mark to make it current. Done clears them.

Trims

Slight −10% · Tighten more −20% · Even sharper −35% · Cut in half −50%. Deletion only — proposed cuts fade in place with the projected word count. Click a faded passage to keep it. Then Make the cuts, Ghost them (reversible), or Walk through one at a time (Cut · Keep · Stop). "Original" clears the trim.

While it thinks

Keep writing; results are found again in the text if it changed. Cancel stops it.

Which model

Passes marked in amber use Claude. Typos and word versions use Sonnet; the rest use the model you choose in Settings (Opus by default).

The rule

The model only ever returns quotes to mark, fixes to accept or cuts to accept; each is located in your text before it's shown, and any it can't find is dropped.

Saving & your documents

Autosave

About a second after you stop typing. ⌘S saves at once. Closing the tab saves on the way out.

Where they're kept

As plain markdown in your Spit n Polish folder (~/Documents/Spit n Polish unless you chose another — Settings shows it). Subfolders work too. Back it up or sync it like any folder.

Files per document

X.md the prose (current versions inline, ghosts as %%…%%) · X.alts.json versions and earlier wordings · X.overflow.md offcuts. The last two appear only when needed.

Other editors at the same time

Open the same file in Obsidian or any editor: changes made there appear here within a few seconds (if you have no unsaved changes). If both changed, nothing is overwritten: a banner offers Load that version or Keep mine and overwrite.

Safe writes

Each save replaces the file in one step, so a crash never leaves half a file. Deleted documents go to the folder's .trash, not oblivion.

Privacy & the AI

Your writing

Stays on your computer. It is sent to Anthropic only when you run an AI feature, and then only the text that feature needs (the selection or the draft, with ghosted text removed).

With Claude Code

If the claude command is installed and signed in, the AI runs on your Claude subscription — no API key, no separate bill, counted against your plan's usage.

With an API key

Otherwise, put ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=… in ~/.config/spitnpolish/env, pip install anthropic, and restart Spit n Polish. Billed to that key: a few cents a pass at most.

After an update

Restart Spit n Polish, then reload the page (⌘R).
